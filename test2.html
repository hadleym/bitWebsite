About Billy Merritt

Billy Merritt is a veteran actor, writer, and producer with over three decades of experience in comedy across New York, Los Angeles, and South Florida.

He’s known for his work at the Upright Citizens Brigade Theatre and for his roles in shows like Reno 911 and Brooklyn Nine-Nine.
